# Auvelle Beauty — Self-Tanning Serum Drops
## 15-Second High-Converting UGC Video Ad Script

**Platform:** TikTok / Instagram Reels  
**Duration:** 15 seconds  
**Style:** UGC — Authentic, fast-paced beauty ad  
**Creator:** Attractive young woman, relatable and confident  
**Higgsfield Preset:** UGC — Realistic Social Media Videos  
**Hook Style:** Spicy (extreme close-up reveal → selfie frame)  
**Setting:** Bathroom (ring light / vanity, getting-ready energy)

## Shot-by-Shot Production Breakdown

### [0–2 sec] — SCROLL-STOPPING HOOK

| Element | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Shot** | Extreme close-up of sun-kissed glowing collarbone / décolletage. Camera tilts slowly upward to reveal dewy, glowing face. |
| **Lighting** | Warm bathroom vanity / ring light — golden, soft. |
| **On-Screen Text** | `"the secret to my summer glow 🤫"` — fades in bottom-center |
| **Audio** | Trending TikTok audio at low volume / soft ambient bathroom background |
| **Voiceover** | *(silent — visual hook only)* |
| **Direction** | No talking. Pure visual intrigue. Skin must look luminous. Slow, confident tilt. |

---

### [2–6 sec] — PRODUCT REVEAL + PROBLEM AGITATION

| Element | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Shot 1** | Pulls back to selfie / front-camera framing — creator in bathroom mirror, holding Auvelle serum drops bottle up to camera |
| **Shot 2** | Quick cut to product close-up — amber dropper bottle, premium label, serum catching light |
| **On-Screen Text** | `"no sun. no spray tan. no mess."` — center screen, bold |
| **Voiceover** | *"Okay, this is literally how I got my tan without laying in the sun."* |
| **Direction** | Confident eye contact with camera. Natural smile. Bottle held at chest height so face + product are both visible. |

---

### [6–10 sec] — DEMO: THE MIX

| Element | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Shot 1** | POV / slight overhead — open palm with dollop of white moisturizer. Dropper bottle tilts in, 2–3 drops of golden serum fall in slow-motion. |
| **Shot 2** | Close-up finger swirling the golden drops into the cream — satisfying mix. |
| **Shot 3** | Creator smoothing mixture onto forearm / shoulder in one sweep. |
| **On-Screen Text** | `"mix a few drops into moisturizer →"` — white text, left-aligned |
| **Voiceover** | *"These are self-tanning serum drops — you just mix a few drops into your moisturizer, apply like normal, and the glow builds naturally."* |
| **Direction** | Keep hands clean and well-lit. Nail polish optional. Slow-mo on the drop falling for maximum ASMR/satisfying visual. |

### [10–13 sec] — THE RESULT: GLOW REVEAL

| Element | Detail |
|---|---|

### [13–15 sec] — CTA: STRONG CLOSE

| Element | Detail |
|---|---|

## Production Notes

- **Aspect Ratio:** 9:16 (TikTok / Reels vertical)
- **Color Grade:** Warm, golden — avoid cool/blue tones
- **Music:** Trending TikTok audio OR upbeat summer instrumental (low under VO)
- **Caption Style:** Bold white text with subtle drop shadow, lowercase = more authentic
- **Pacing:** Fast cuts at 2–3 seconds each. No lingering shots except the slow-mo drop.
- **Creator Energy:** Confident but casual. Feels like she's telling a friend, not selling to a stranger.
